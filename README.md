# Customer-Product-Analytics

A focused analytics project that studies how **customer behavior** and **product/service usage** relate to business outcomes through exploratory analysis.

## Focus Areas

### 1) Customer
Analyze customer attributes and lifecycle signals such as:
- Demographics and profile context
- Tenure and engagement behavior
- Satisfaction and complaint patterns

### 2) Product
Understand how product/service adoption affects customer outcomes:
- Number of subscribed services
- Feature/add-on usage patterns
- Product mix differences across customer groups

### 3) Analytics
Use data analysis to uncover patterns and support decisions:
- Distribution and imbalance analysis
- Missing value and data quality checks
- Correlation and relationship exploration
- Segment-level comparisons and visual insights

## Repository Contents

- `Customer_Churn_Prediction_Dataset_EDA.ipynb`  
  Exploratory analysis of customer churn with strong emphasis on customer experience and product/service usage.

- `Mall Customer Segmentation: AI/Mall_Customer_Segmentation_Phyo_Thaw.ipynb`  
  Customer segmentation analysis to identify distinct customer groups and behavioral patterns.

## Typical Analytics Workflow

1. Load and inspect customer-product data
2. Validate data quality (nulls, duplicates, outliers)
3. Analyze customer and product behavior patterns
4. Compare key metrics across segments and outcomes
5. Summarize actionable insights for business decisions

This repository is designed to provide practical, data-driven insight into:
